## Overview

**Mobile design exploration · Sole designer · 3 months**

LUNA explores how a mobile interface can make personal health information easier to interpret. I developed the product direction, information architecture, visual system, and prototype flows in Figma.

The design focuses on contextual summaries and a supportive tone. Its effects on comprehension and emotional comfort remain hypotheses for user testing.

## The Design Question

A dashboard can show many measurements without helping someone understand what deserves attention. I explored how to provide an overview first, with detail available when the person wants it.

<Figure src="/work/intuitive-data-visualization.png" alt="LUNA design exploration translating personal data into visual summaries" caption="Design exploration: visual summaries as an entry point into more detailed information." />

## Key Design Decisions

### Prioritize a small set of relevant summaries

A modular dashboard gives each card a clear job and reduces the amount of information competing for attention.

**Trade-off:** simplification hides detail. The next interaction must make that detail easy to find, and the summary must retain enough context to be useful.

<Figure src="/work/adaptive-dashboard.png" alt="LUNA modular mobile dashboard design" caption="Dashboard design: modular cards establish an information hierarchy for the small screen." />

### Use abstraction carefully

The visual body-awareness exploration uses simplified representations instead of realistic body imagery. The intention is to support reflection without prescribing an ideal appearance.

**Trade-off:** abstract visuals can also be ambiguous. Their meaning and emotional effect need to be checked with the intended audience.

<Figure src="/work/visual-body-awareness-model.png" alt="LUNA simplified visual body-awareness design" caption="Visual-language exploration: an abstract representation intended for a supportive mobile experience." />

## Outcome & Reflection

The deliverables are interface designs, reusable component patterns, and prototype flows. The project shaped my approach to information density, progressive disclosure, and tone on mobile.

Next, I would test whether people can explain the summaries accurately, locate the underlying detail, and describe how the visual language makes them feel. Those observations should guide refinement before making claims about engagement or wellbeing.
